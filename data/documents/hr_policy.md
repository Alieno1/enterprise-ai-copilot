# HR Policy

## Leave Policy

Employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Leave requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days before the planned leave.

## Work From Home

Employees may request up to 2 work-from-home days per week, subject to manager approval.

Work-from-home requests should be submitted through the HR portal.

## Employee Onboarding

New employees must complete their onboarding checklist within the first 10 working days.

The onboarding checklist includes:

- Identity verification
- Bank account details
- Emergency contact information
- Security training
- IT equipment acknowledgment

## HR Support

HR support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM.

Employees can contact HR through the internal HR portal for questions regarding leave, benefits, payroll, and employment policies.
