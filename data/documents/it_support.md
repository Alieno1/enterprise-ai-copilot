# IT Support Policy

## Password Reset

Employees who forget their corporate password can request a password reset through the IT service desk.

Password reset requests should include:

- Employee ID
- Corporate email address
- Description of the issue

For security reasons, employees must never share their existing password with IT support.

## Laptop Issues

For hardware or laptop problems, employees should create an IT support ticket.

The ticket should include:

- Employee ID
- Device asset ID
- Description of the problem
- Error message, if applicable

Critical hardware failures should be marked as high priority.

## IT Support Hours

Standard IT support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Emergency IT support is available for critical business-impacting incidents outside standard support hours.

## Ticket Priority

- Low: General questions or non-urgent requests.
- Medium: Issues affecting an employee's normal workflow.
- High: Issues preventing important business operations.
- Critical: Issues affecting critical business services or multiple employees.
